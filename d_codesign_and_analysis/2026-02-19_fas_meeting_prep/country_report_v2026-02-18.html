▶ About the Scenarios

Data notes: Temperature data from CESM2-WACCM6 model runs (Toon et al. 2019 for 5–47 Tg; Coupe et al. for 150 Tg). Agriculture (CLM) from crop models driven by CESM climate output. Starvation estimates from Xia et al. 2022 food modeling. Values shown are country-area-weighted averages. Scenarios represent black carbon (soot) injected into stratosphere; higher values correspond to larger nuclear exchanges.
